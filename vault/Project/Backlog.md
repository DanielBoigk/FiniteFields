## V — Vault content

- **V0 — Restructure the vault.** Folders by subject (proposal: `Fields/`, `Characteristic 2/`,
  `Prime Fields/`, `Extension Fields/`, `Linear Algebra/`, `Analysis/`, `Category Theory/`,
  `Comparisons/`, `Algorithms/`), a `Map of Content.md`, a concept-note template in
  `vault/templates/`, by-name wikilinks.
- **V1 — Foundations.** One note each: field; characteristic; prime field; $\mathbb{F}_p$ as
  `ZMod p`; finite field; order is a prime power; existence and uniqueness of $\mathbb{F}_{p^n}$
  (`GaloisField`); multiplicative group is cyclic; primitive element; Frobenius; subfield lattice
  ↔ divisibility of $n$; Galois group is cyclic generated by Frobenius; trace and norm; minimal
  polynomial; irreducible polynomials and counting them; Conway polynomials.
- **V2 — Characteristic 2 (priority).** $\mathbb{F}_2$ and Boolean arithmetic; $-1 = 1$ and
  addition is XOR; polynomial basis of $\mathbb{F}_{2^n}$; every element is a square and the
  square-root map; Artin–Schreier equation $x^2 + x = c$ and the trace criterion; normal bases;
  tower fields; quadratic forms in characteristic 2 (symmetric ≠ alternating, Arf invariant);
  standard fields $\mathbb{F}_{2^8}$ (AES), $\mathbb{F}_{2^{128}}$ (GHASH); examples worked by hand.
- **V3 — Categorical view.** Fields have no products and no initial object; the category of finite
  fields of characteristic $p$ and embeddings (hom-sets nonempty iff $m \mid n$, then of size $m$);
  $\overline{\mathbb{F}}_p$ as a colimit; absolute Galois group $\hat{\mathbb{Z}}$; finite étale
  algebras ↔ finite continuous $\hat{\mathbb{Z}}$-sets (Galois categories,
  `Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Galois/`); Frobenius as a natural transformation; `ModuleCat` over
  $\mathbb{F}_q$.
- **V4 — Comparisons with $\mathbb{R}$ and $\mathbb{C}$.** No ordering; only the trivial absolute
  value; discrete topology and no limits; **scalar products**: no `InnerProductSpace`, but
  bilinear/hermitian forms, isotropic vectors, failure of Gram–Schmidt, orthogonal complements
  that meet the space; **differentiability**: formal derivative, $(x^p)' = 0$, Hasse derivatives
  and Taylor expansion in characteristic $p$; Fourier analysis via additive characters
  (Walsh–Hadamard in characteristic 2); what "analysis over $\mathbb{F}_q$" means (scope of FF-004).
- **V5 — Linear algebra over $\mathbb{F}_q$** (was FF-003): counting subspaces, $GL_n(\mathbb{F}_q)$
  order, rank, solving; specification before code.

## L — Lean

- **L0 — Library layout** under `lean/FiniteFields/` mirroring the vault subjects; one file per
  subject, each linked from its notes.
- **L1 — Characteristic 2:** statements for V2 (squares, Artin–Schreier trace criterion,
  `CharTwo` lemmas), reusing `Mathlib/Algebra/CharP/Two.lean` and `FieldTheory/Finite/*`.
- **L2 — Categorical statements** for V3, beside the classical ones.
- **L3 — Generalisation survey:** list mathlib results stated only for $\mathbb{R}$, $\mathbb{C}$
  or `RCLike` that hold over any field, then restate them generically.
- **L4 — Proof sweep:** discharge the sorry ledger.

## J — Julia (`FiniteFieldArithmetic.jl`)

- **J0 — Package skeleton, test first:** `test/` with Aqua.jl, JET.jl, DispatchDoctor.jl;
  `bench/` environment with a benchmark suite; CI.
- **J1 — $\mathbb{F}_{2^n}$ elements** in the smallest fitting `UInt` (`UInt8` … `UInt128`),
  hex printing, `+` as XOR.
- **J2 — Multiplication strategies**, selected by dispatch and compared by benchmark: shift-and-add,
  log/antilog tables (small $n$), carry-less multiplication (x86 `PCLMULQDQ`, ARM `PMULL` via LLVM
  intrinsics), reduction for sparse polynomials; profiled.
- **J3 — Inversion and division:** Fermat, extended Euclid, Itoh–Tsujii; benchmarked.
- **J4 — $\mathbb{F}_p$** in the smallest fitting `UInt`; Barrett/Montgomery as options.
- **J5 — $\mathbb{F}_{p^n}$ generic.**
